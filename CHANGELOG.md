### Added

- 5 reasoning strategies: sequential, dialectic, parallel, analogical, abductive
- Multi-factor confidence scoring
- Self-critique with severity levels

- Thought pruning: dead-end detection, redundancy removal, path optimization
- 6 MCP tools: think, evaluate, metacog, graph, prune, reset
- 118 unit tests
